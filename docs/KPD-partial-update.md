# Partial material, composite choices and product photo

KPD Premier Partial is described consistently as Flexible PMMA with Gingival
Composite. The base is 100% clear flexible PMMA. New final-partial orders offer
Pink or Meharry gingival composite, with no composite surcharge.

$250 and $325 per arch respectively. Selecting teeth in both arches doubles the
base price; additional teeth in the same arch do not. Optional printed models
retain their $15 per arch charge. Rush production and shipping remain separate.

including keyboard form submission. Final partials require one of the two new
composite options. The stored gum-shade values include the material and fit the
existing 50-character field. Historical records and other product forms are not
rewritten; no database migration is needed.

description, pricing page, removable-program description, and order selection
card/form. Retired Acetal comments and TCS wording/imports have been removed from
